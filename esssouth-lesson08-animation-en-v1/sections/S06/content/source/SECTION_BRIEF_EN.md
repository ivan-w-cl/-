## L08.S06.B04: Leave the Next Turn Open

Scene family: handover_after_rehearsal

### L08.S06.c010 · coach

**Visual:** End with a proposed check and an unfilled reply slot; do not animate guaranteed acceptance.

**Phrase anchor:** use the actual reply
