## L03.S06.B01: Understand Before You Repair

Scene family: formal_setup

### L03.S06.c001 · coach

Even when you are listening carefully, you may respond to the wrong part of a sentence. A correction gives you another chance to understand. The useful move is to update your understanding before defending the reply you already gave.

**Visual:** Show a provisional Understanding card that can be edited, not a broken learner or red failure icon.

**Phrase anchor:** update your understanding

### L03.S06.c002 · coach

We will use an authored example from the same workshop discussion. The group has thirty minutes and one facilitator. The following misunderstanding belongs to the example. It is not something we have heard you say.

**Visual:** Re-establish task facts and display Authored example before any cast appears. No learner recording or response data.

**Phrase anchor:** not something we have heard you say

### L03.S06.d001 · noah

If several pairs lose their place at once, one facilitator may not reach everyone.

**Visual:** Noah states a conditional support concern; show possible concurrent requests inside a clearly labelled If frame.

**Phrase anchor:** one facilitator

### L03.S06.d002 · learner

Could we put more paper on each table?

**Visual:** The model learner proposes additional paper. Show this in a separate material-supply lane, not as an action the real student chose.

**Phrase anchor:** more paper

### L03.S06.d003 · noah

I mean help with the steps, not a shortage of paper.

**Visual:** Noah calmly points back to the help lane. Do not animate anger, shame or rejection of the learner as a person.

**Phrase anchor:** help with the steps

## L03.S06.B02: Revise the Meaning First

Scene family: formal_correction_analysis

### L03.S06.c003 · coach

The first reply treated the problem as a shortage of materials. Noah has now made the issue clearer: several pairs might need help with the instructions at the same time. More paper does not address that concern.

**Visual:** Move the provisional understanding card from the materials lane to the support lane. Preserve the quote so the reason for the move is visible.

**Phrase anchor:** at the same time

### L03.S06.c004 · coach

A brief acknowledgement can mark the update. Thanks for clarifying is one option. It is useful only if the next words reflect the corrected meaning. You do not need a long apology, and you do not need to pretend that you agree with every proposed solution.

**Visual:** Show Thanks for clarifying as a small optional phrase attached to the updated meaning, never as a mandatory first box.

**Phrase anchor:** corrected meaning

### L03.S06.c005 · coach

Before you rehearse, identify the concern in your own words. What could happen if several pairs need help, and why does having one facilitator matter? Keep that issue separate from the question of how much paper is available.

**Visual:** Use the formal two-lane board and a blank thought area, with only the task and corrected quote retained.

**Phrase anchor:** one facilitator matter

### L03.S06.h101 · hold 10 s

Privately identify the corrected meaning.

**Visual:** Private area stays blank; no automatic inference about understanding.

## L03.S06.B03: A Private Spoken Attempt

Scene family: self_paced_rehearsal

### L03.S06.c006 · coach

Now form a short English reply to Noah's correction. You could acknowledge the clarification, show which concern you now understand, and suggest or ask about one relevant next step. Use only the parts you need. This is private rehearsal: nothing is recorded or scored.

**Visual:** Show the exact correction and optional response resources, not a fill-in formula. No microphone, transcript field or assessment.

**Phrase anchor:** Use only the parts you need

### L03.S06.c007 · coach

In Watch mode, there is a short time to try before one possible reply is shown. In Practice mode, take as long as you need, then choose the model or skip it. Seeing the model does not mean that you have completed the speaking task.

**Visual:** Show a quiet mode note before entering the gate; mode-specific controls will be readable DOM outside the stage.

**Phrase anchor:** does not mean that you have completed

## L03.S06.B04: Review Without Assuming Performance

Scene family: shared_analysis

### L03.S06.c008 · coach

Whether you tried a reply, watched the model, or skipped it, the same question is useful: did the response address the concern that was actually stated? You can check a reply you remember, or simply use the question for your next attempt.

**Visual:** Use neutral common continuation identical after model or skip. Do not show a copied model as the user's response.

**Phrase anchor:** or skipped it

### L03.S06.c009 · coach

Notice also whether the reply adds an assumption. Saying you are worried about not having enough materials would keep the original misunderstanding. Saying you want us to cancel the workshop would add a claim Noah did not make.

**Visual:** Display these as explicitly labelled inaccurate interpretations, each next to the exact correction. Avoid personality judgements.

**Phrase anchor:** a claim Noah did not make

### L03.S06.c010 · coach

You may still disagree about what to do next. Understanding the concern does not commit you to a particular plan. For now, keep the two questions separate: what does the person mean, and what response would help the group examine it?

**Visual:** Reveal separate Understanding and Next move cards. This section does not expand into negotiation or winning a disagreement.

**Phrase anchor:** does not commit you

### L03.S06.h102 · hold 8 s

Choose one feature to check privately.

**Visual:** Leave both questions visible without ticking either or claiming mastery.

### L03.S06.c011 · coach

If you want another attempt, return to the rehearsal and change one thing: make the corrected concern clearer, remove an added assumption, or make the next question more specific. You choose what needs attention. The player cannot judge a reply it has not heard.

**Visual:** End with optional review resources and normal transport; no forced retry, new recording or automatic advancement.

**Phrase anchor:** cannot judge a reply it has not heard
