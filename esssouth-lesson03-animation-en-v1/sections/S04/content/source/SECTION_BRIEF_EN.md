## L03.S04.B04: Avoid Automatic Extra Sentences

Scene family: contextual_choice

### L03.S04.c008 · coach

Do not add a new idea simply to make the reply longer. If the group has already checked a detail and is confirming the arrangement, a clear yes may be all that is needed. The value of your response depends on the moment.

**Visual:** Show a separate explicitly hypothetical confirmation panel. Do not alter the open main-case decision state.

**Phrase anchor:** depends on the moment

### L03.S04.c009 · coach

Before adding something, ask yourself: which point am I responding to, and what work will the extra detail do? You might clarify a method, identify a limit, or ask a question. You do not have to do all three.

**Visual:** Return to the main board; reveal two diagnostic questions, with optional examples underneath. No mandatory sequence numbers.

**Phrase anchor:** do not have to do all three
