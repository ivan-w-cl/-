# Watch and Practice

## Shared rules

Use one authoritative graph. Authored continuations illustrate consequences and questions in a fictional discussion; they are not predictions of real reactions or measurements of a learner. No branch supplies scores or confirms mastery. The underlying practice session has not happened, and the proposed use of the tool has not been adopted.

S01/S04/S05/S07 share their stated reflections and explanation in both modes. Do not add an extra example after every hold. Where an authored model appears, keep it separate from the blank private-response area.

## Choice gates: L04.I01 in S02 and L04.I02 in S03

Follow the exact prompt, options, branch cues, thinking hold and common rejoin in each section JSON. Watch demonstrates I01=B and I02=C, never all mutually exclusive alternatives in a continuous exchange. In Practice, each alternative is available from the same restored baseline.

State flow: awaiting_choice -> branch_playing -> branch_ready -> common continuation. Waiting is indefinite. Play, Space, rate changes or elapsed background time do not choose an answer. Ignore repeated selections while a branch is active. At branch end, wait for Continue; rejoin the common cue exactly once. Retry clears the prior outcome and restores the original prompt. Skip remains unanswered and rejoins without praise or correctness.

Common narration must remain valid after A, B, C and Skip. If a later worked example requires information stated only in a branch, establish it afresh in a clearly separate example. Do not refer to an unplayed branch as if the student heard it.

Seeking across an unvisited gate stops at the gate. Seeking before it resets the attempt. Branch-local seeking cannot spill into another branch. Mode switching within a gate resets to that mode's entry; outside it, map the common cue and offset where possible.

## Clock, transport and integration

Use one active playback clock. Pause, seek, restart, mode and section switches cancel earlier scheduling. Future audio cannot leak past an unvisited gate. Keep 1/1.25/1.5/2 rates consistent with captions and media time; preserve pitch when actual recorded audio is later attached. Silent tests establish transport and timing, not audible quality.

Show common section progress with separate branch/model progress when needed. Unbounded decision time is not fabricated elapsed course time. A combined player must dispose of old section clocks, listeners and future audio when switching sections. No automatic next-section departure from a waiting interaction and no purchase gate.
