## L04.S02.B02: Separate Recognition from Agreement

Scene family: honesty_and_specificity

### L04.S02.c004 · coach

A phrase such as I see why time matters recognises the reason. I agree that we should use it immediately accepts the proposed action. These are different commitments, even if both sound friendly.

**Visual:** Compare two clearly labelled authored phrases, not a viewer transcript. Link each to its exact object: the reason or the proposal.

**Phrase anchor:** These are different commitments

## L04.S02.B04: Keep Both Sides of the Reply Accurate

Scene family: shared_explanation

### L04.S02.c010 · coach

**Visual:** All paths rejoin here once. Reconstruct the two task-related considerations from common facts, without assuming an option was chosen or a peer continuation was heard.

**Phrase anchor:** neither settles the method on its own

### L04.S02.c011 · coach

**Visual:** Show two meaningful connections to the source rather than a fixed compliment-then-objection template. No automatic praise or emotional scoring.

**Phrase anchor:** Keep both specific
