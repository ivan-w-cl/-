## L04.S01.B03: Make Direct Disagreement About the Proposal

Scene family: brief_respectful_example

### L04.S01.c008 · coach

Direct disagreement can be respectful. The important distinction is between challenging a proposal and judging the person who offered it. A calm voice does not replace a reason, and a clear objection need not be disguised as agreement.

**Visual:** Separate a proposal-focused lane from personal judgements. Do not assign moral identities to the cast or represent politeness as a winning score.

**Phrase anchor:** need not be disguised as agreement

### L04.S01.c010 · coach

**Phrase anchor:** without inventing a hidden motive
