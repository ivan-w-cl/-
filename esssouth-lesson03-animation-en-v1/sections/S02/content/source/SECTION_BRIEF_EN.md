## L03.S02.B04: Keep the Check Proportionate

Scene family: shared_meaning_analysis

### L03.S02.c010 · coach

A useful check preserves the point you need to understand. In this exchange, that includes a brief demonstration, time to try, and the possibility of missing a step. None cancels the others.

**Visual:** All paths enter here once. Reconstruct essential source meaning from the original line, without assuming which branch was viewed or that a choice was made.

**Phrase anchor:** None cancels the others

### L03.S02.c011 · coach

When the meaning is already clear, you can respond directly. When a small detail is uncertain, check that detail. Repeating an entire contribution may use time without improving understanding.

**Visual:** Show three contexts: clear meaning, one uncertain detail, whole point uncertain. Keep them alternatives chosen by context, not a numbered ritual.

**Phrase anchor:** check that detail

### L03.S02.c012 · coach

A check should also leave room for correction. You are offering your reading, not telling someone what they must mean. Their reply may confirm it, narrow it or change your understanding.

**Visual:** Keep the interpretation card editable and the source intact. Three neutral response directions remain possibilities, without predicted facial reactions or scores.

**Phrase anchor:** offering your reading
