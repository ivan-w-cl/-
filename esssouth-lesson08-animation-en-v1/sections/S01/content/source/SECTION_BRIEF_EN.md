## L08.S01.B03: Separate a Limit from a Preference

Scene family: constraint_and_status_board

### L08.S01.c006 · coach

**Visual:** Move the five-minute minimum to the fixed-constraint rail while candidate stages remain on a proposed sequence rail.

## L08.S01.B04: Use the Map to Start a Check

Scene family: summary_to_check

### L08.S01.c009 · coach

Before combining anything, check your map against what was actually said. Have you kept the main proposal, the reason that was supplied, the fixed limits and the unresolved question? You do not need four boxes in every real conversation; you need enough structure to avoid losing a material distinction.

**Visual:** Reduce the full map to a compact inspection card; four categories are optional aids, not a compulsory speech formula.

**Phrase anchor:** a material distinction

### L08.S01.c010 · coach

The next step is not to announce that everyone agrees. It is to ask which parts already have explicit support and which still need discussion. A clear map gives the group something concrete to correct before you invest in a combined plan.

**Visual:** End with the map becoming an editable draft summary, labelled Ready to check, not Agreed plan.

**Phrase anchor:** something concrete to correct
