# Curriculum context

The series has ten core lessons. The preview is separate, and Watch/Practice are two modes of each lesson rather than additional lessons.

The current progression is: L01 shared discussion and useful contributions; L02 views, reasons and examples with limits; L03 listening and connected responses; L04 constructive disagreement and proposal revision; L05 reading a prompt and preparing a first contribution; L06 specific experience, bounded outcomes and follow-ups; L07 pauses, overlap, invitations and sidetracks.

L08 now brings several contributions into a coherent candidate and a faithful group summary. It is not a repeat of L04 defending or revising one person's view, and not L07's restoration of a stalled exchange. Its distinct output is an accurately scoped synthesis with a useful next check.

L09 is planned to focus on adapting the learned methods to unfamiliar tasks. L10 is planned to use a continuous teaching discussion with review. These later details are not authoritative input to this animation job.

The Lesson 08 title and seven-section mapping are newly authored under the requested continuation, not recovered previously approved scripts. Existing approval applies to the retained animation style, language separation and teaching rhythm. This package supplies the exact new script to implement.
