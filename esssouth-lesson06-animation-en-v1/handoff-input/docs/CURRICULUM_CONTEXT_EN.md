# Curriculum context

The series is planned as ten core lessons, each with an editorial allocation of roughly thirty minutes. Preview/sample extracts are separate. Watch and Practice are two modes of the same lesson, not additional lessons.

Current production sequence:

1. L01: understand shared discussion and ways to contribute.
2. L02: develop a view, reason and example with appropriate limits.
3. L03: listen closely, check meaning and respond to the point.
4. L04: disagree constructively and continue examining alternatives.
5. L05: interpret a prompt, prepare useful notes and make a relevant first contribution.
6. L06: describe a specific personal experience, distinguish one's contribution, explain decisions, report bounded outcomes and answer follow-ups.

Earlier curriculum notes used a different order. This handoff supplies the proposed complete L06 script in the current sequence, not a recovery of an earlier numbered script. It is whole Lesson 06, not Section 06 of another lesson.

Remaining coverage is planned for disruptions to discussion, combining contributions into a shared proposal, transfer to unfamiliar tasks, and a continuous teaching simulation with review. Detailed scripts for those lessons are not part of this assignment.

## Progression within this lesson

S01 chooses one concrete event. S02 separates individual from shared contribution. S03 explains a decision rather than only naming an action. S04 distinguishes observed outcomes from unsupported causal impact. S05 answers a particular follow-up and makes a useful, proposed reflection. S06 rehearses privately without recording or assessment. S07 changes the question and routes different parts of the same fact bank to it, without changing history.

Use the visitor-guide case consistently for continuity. It is explicitly fictional. Learners may use the model for labelled language practice; their own accounts must remain their own. The lesson does not require an exceptional achievement, private identifying details, a fixed answer length or a compulsory formula. These are communication teaching choices, not official assessment rules.

The English silent review leaves production coaching and feedback editable for the later localization/audio pass. Do not alter the accepted visual language while preparing those future hooks.
