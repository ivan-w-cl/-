# Lesson 04: Disagree Constructively and Move the Discussion Forward

This is the proposed next lesson. Its learning progression is new; its established visual style and lecture-first pacing are retained.

| Section | Title | Editorial allocation | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| L04.S01 | Locate the Point of Disagreement | 3:30 | Identify the specific proposal, reason or unresolved detail being challenged; disagree with a claim without inventing a motive or rejecting every related idea. |
| L04.S02 | Recognise a Reason Without Pretending to Agree | 4:30 | Acknowledge a genuinely relevant reason while stating a real reservation; distinguish recognition from agreement and avoid using personal judgements as objections. |
| L04.S03 | Explain the Concern Precisely | 4:30 | State a task-related concern with a clear condition and possible consequence; distinguish a precise objection from vague politeness or an unsupported universal claim. |
| L04.S04 | Compare Options Against the Same Criteria | 4:30 | Compare two concrete proposals using the same task-relevant criteria, while separating possible advantages from unverified outcomes. |
| L04.S05 | Revise a Proposal Without Inventing Agreement | 4:30 | Revise a proposal in response to a specific concern and record only the agreement actually expressed, leaving unanswered questions visible. |
| L04.S06 | Rehearse a Constructive Disagreement | 5:30 | Privately rehearse a clear, respectful disagreement that addresses the actual proposal, explains a relevant concern and leaves the group a concrete question or alternative to examine. |
| L04.S07 | Transfer and Review | 3:00 | Express a task-relevant disagreement in a new setting, propose a way to examine it, and distinguish procedural agreement from agreement on the final choice. |

Total editorial allocation: 30 minutes. Derived English silent reading estimate: **1746.806 seconds**, using 140 spoken words per minute, a 0.35-second tail per cue, and the explicit holds. This is not a measured animation or a promise about the future voiced runtime. Do not add filler to force 30:00.

S02 and S03 contain meaningful authored choice gates. S06 contains self-paced private rehearsal of a constructive disagreement. The other sections share reflection and explanation in both modes. Watch chooses one declared branch at each gate; it does not play every mutually exclusive branch. Practice time includes unbounded learner time and depends on the selected branch.

The fictional practice-tool scenario is shared for continuity, while each section re-establishes the facts it needs. S07 transfers to an event-notice layout discussion. Acknowledgement, a precise concern, a comparison or a next step are available resources, not a compulsory sentence formula. Neither immediate help nor unaided effort is always the right choice; examine the task and conditions.
