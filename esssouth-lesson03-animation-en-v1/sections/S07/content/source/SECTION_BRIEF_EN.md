## L03.S07.B01: Hear a New Contribution

Scene family: new_task

### L03.S07.c001 · coach

Try the same listening approach in a new discussion. A group is planning a short guide for new club members. They want it to be easy to read and useful to someone who does not already know how the club works. No version has been chosen.

**Visual:** Introduce a fresh blank guide with task cards. Do not reuse a finished workshop outcome or display a published guide.

**Phrase anchor:** No version has been chosen

### L03.S07.d001 · maya

**Visual:** Brief cast example with the statement visible in full. The concern is terminology, not evidence that any guide has failed.

**Phrase anchor:** some new members may not know the terms

Separate the benefit Maya suggests from the concern she raises. Do not turn the possibility that some new members may not know the terms into a claim that they cannot understand anything. Do not assume she wants a longer guide unless she says so.

**Visual:** Move the exact phrase some new members and the word may into a scope highlight. The word some qualifies the people, not a newly assumed subset of terms. Keep a hypothetical Longer guide interpretation outside the evidence area.

**Phrase anchor:** unless she says so
