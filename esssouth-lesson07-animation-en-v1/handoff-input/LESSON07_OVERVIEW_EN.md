# Lesson 07: Keep the Discussion Moving When It Gets Stuck

This is the proposed next lesson. Its learning progression is new; its established visual style and lecture-first pacing are retained.

| Section | Title | Editorial allocation | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| L07.S01 | Notice What the Discussion Needs | 3:30 | Use observable words and turn-taking to identify a small next need; distinguish an unfinished task connection from assumed agreement, ignorance, hostility or a permanent participant trait. |
| L07.S02 | Restart After a Pause | 4:30 | Use a pause after a proposal as a context to observe and respond to, without inferring agreement or a motive; compare a focused open question with a premature decision or pressured invitation. |
| L07.S03 | Handle Overlapping Turns | 4:30 | Resolve an overlap by making room for a turn and preserving an unfinished point, without assuming hostile intent, treating turn-taking permission as agreement or combining proposals before they are understood. |
| L07.S04 | Invite a Contribution Without Pressure | 4:30 | Offer a relevant invitation that leaves the other person free to contribute, pass or come in later, without inferring motives or assigning equal speaking quotas. |
| L07.S05 | Bring a Sidetrack Back to the Task | 4:30 | Check whether a side topic contributes to the stated task, then redirect to a specific unresolved issue without dismissing creative ideas or inventing a discussion deadline. |
| L07.S06 | Rehearse a Small Repair | 5:30 | Privately rehearse one task-linked next move after a brief pause, leaving space for the actual reply and avoiding claims that the plan or discussion is already resolved. |
| L07.S07 | Choose the Next Move in a New Discussion | 3:00 | Choose a context-sensitive next move in a fresh task, using explicit words and relevance rather than a memorised intervention or guessed motive. |

Total editorial allocation: 30 minutes. Derived English silent reading estimate: **1680.791 seconds**, using 140 spoken words per minute, a 0.35-second tail per cue, and the explicit holds. This is not a measured animation or a promise about the future voiced runtime. Do not add filler to force 30:00.

S02 and S03 contain authored choices about a pause and overlapping turns. S06 offers private rehearsal of one small repair. Watch runs through the declared branch/model automatically; Practice waits for a choice and explicit continuation. The other sections share reflection and explanation in both modes. Watch chooses one declared branch at each gate; it does not play every mutually exclusive branch. Practice time includes unbounded learner time and depends on the selected branch.

S01–S06 share a welcome-activity prompt, but each demonstration starts from its declared excerpt baseline. Do not carry a branch outcome into another excerpt or turn the cast into fixed quiet, disruptive or controlling personalities. S07 changes to a reusable-mug discussion to practise choosing a next move from new evidence. A reply is a possible continuation, not proof that a repair worked or a plan was adopted.
