## L04.S07.B01: A New Task and a Real Trade-off

Scene family: fresh_task_and_proposal

### L04.S07.c001 · coach

Try a different discussion. A team is planning a one-page event notice for a community room. They want people to notice the event and easily find its time and place. No layout has been chosen.

**Visual:** Introduce an unfilled single-page outline and the two task aims. Do not show an attractive finished winner or claim any audience has seen it.

**Phrase anchor:** No layout has been chosen

### L04.S07.d001 · noah

A large picture could catch people's attention. I would use most of the page for it.

**Visual:** Short character example. The picture allocation is a proposal inside a page outline, not an installed layout. Retain could in the exact quote.

**Phrase anchor:** could catch

### L04.S07.c002 · coach

You can recognise the possible benefit without accepting that amount of space. If the information becomes difficult to find, that would conflict with another aim. We have not tested a layout, so keep the concern conditional.

**Visual:** Separate possible attention benefit from proposed page allocation. Show an If frame around the readability concern. Do not depict invisible tiny details as an already observed failure.

**Phrase anchor:** keep the concern conditional

## L04.S07.B03: Examine One Possible Continuation

**Visual:** Noah agrees only to comparing. Split the visible quote into accepted next action and continuing preference. Do not animate a final handshake or winner badge.

**Phrase anchor:** I still think

### L04.S07.c004 · coach

The reply identifies a specific concern and offers a way to examine it. Noah agrees to the comparison in this example, but has not chosen a layout. His continuing preference is not a failure of the conversation.

**Visual:** Return to a formal two-column record of what was agreed and what remains open. Preserve the conditional link; this is an authored exchange, not the learner's measured result.

### L04.S07.c005 · coach

**Visual:** Show two neutral empty candidate frames, with no scores or superior appearance. Keep time/place criteria visible. Do not fill a fictional evidence table with fabricated ratings.

**Phrase anchor:** cannot conclude

## L04.S07.B04: Keep One Useful Check

Scene family: personal_review_and_close

### L04.S07.c006 · coach

If you formed a reply, review one feature: was the disagreement specific, was the reason connected to the task, or did the next step make something examinable? If you did not rehearse, choose a feature to try next time.

**Visual:** Show three optional review resources without ticks, score or automatic selection; private area is not replaced with the model.

**Phrase anchor:** If you formed a reply

### L04.S07.h102 · hold 8 s

Select a personal focus for a future attempt.

**Visual:** Keep all resources unselected. No inferred completion or claim of improvement.

### L04.S07.c007 · coach

You can disagree directly and respectfully. You do not have to manufacture agreement, force a compromise, or use the same three-part sentence every time. Keep the issue clear enough for others to examine. You can revisit the examples or try the rehearsal again.

**Visual:** Neutral finish with normal user-controlled navigation. No auto-advance, purchase gate, mastery claim or mandatory slogan.
