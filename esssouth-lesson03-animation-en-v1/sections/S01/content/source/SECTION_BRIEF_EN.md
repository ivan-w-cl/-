## L03.S01.B03: Hear a Concern Without Expanding It

### L03.S01.c008 · coach

**Phrase anchor:** Noah names a possibility

### L03.S01.c009 · coach

**Visual:** Place an unresolved support question beside the proposal. No answer card is prefilled and no facilitator action is enacted.

**Phrase anchor:** keep that question open

### L03.S01.c010 · coach

**Visual:** Use a boundary between stated content and interpretations. Avoid facial expressions suggesting anxiety, hostility or certainty not supplied by the line.
