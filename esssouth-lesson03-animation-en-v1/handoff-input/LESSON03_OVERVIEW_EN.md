# Lesson 03: Listen Closely, Respond to the Point

This is the proposed next lesson. Its learning progression is new; its established visual style and lecture-first pacing are retained.

| Section | Title | Editorial allocation | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| L03.S01 | Hear the View, Reason, and Concern | 3:30 | Identify what a speaker proposes, the reason offered and an expressed concern; preserve uncertainty and separate what was said from interpretation. |
| L03.S02 | Check Your Understanding | 4:30 | Use a brief, faithful paraphrase when a check is useful; retain the speaker’s purpose and uncertainty without adding a stronger claim. |
| L03.S03 | Ask the Question That Matters | 4:30 | Locate a consequential ambiguity and ask a focused clarification before giving a targeted response; distinguish clarification from repeating known information or advancing an ungrounded interpretation. |
| L03.S04 | Agree and Add Something Relevant | 4:30 | Make clear which part of a contribution you support, then add a relevant detail only if the discussion needs one. |
| L03.S05 | Respond to the Concern Within the Task | 4:30 | Address the actual concern without losing the shared objective or treating a partial response as a complete solution. |
| L03.S06 | Rehearse, Hear a Correction, and Adjust | 5:30 | Acknowledge a correction, update the understood concern, and offer a relevant next move without pretending that the learner has been recorded or assessed. |
| L03.S07 | Transfer and Review | 3:00 | Select a relevant listening or response move in a new context, without applying a rigid sequence or implying assessed mastery. |

Total editorial allocation: 30 minutes. Derived English silent reading estimate: **1756.295 seconds**, using 140 spoken words per minute, a 0.35-second tail per cue, and the explicit holds. This is not a measured animation or a promise about the future voiced runtime. Do not add filler to force 30:00.

S02 and S03 contain meaningful authored choice gates. S06 contains self-paced private rehearsal. The other sections share reflection and explanation in both modes. Watch chooses one declared branch at each gate; it does not play every mutually exclusive branch. Practice time includes unbounded learner time and depends on the selected branch.

The workshop scenario is shared for continuity but each section re-establishes the facts it needs. S07 transfers to a fresh guide-planning discussion. A concise paraphrase, clarification, agreement or extension is useful when the moment calls for it; these are resources rather than a required sentence formula.
