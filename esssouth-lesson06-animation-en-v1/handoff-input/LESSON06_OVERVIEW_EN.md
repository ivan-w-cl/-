# Lesson 06: Explain Your Experience and Respond to Follow-Up Questions

This is the proposed next lesson. Its learning progression is new; its established visual style and lecture-first pacing are retained.

| Section | Title | Editorial allocation | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| L06.S01 | Choose One Specific Experience | 3:30 | Choose a specific, truthful episode with a clear action or decision to discuss; distinguish a useful experience account from a broad claim about personal qualities, without inventing achievements or disclosing private details. |
| L06.S02 | Make Your Own Contribution Clear | 4:30 | Make personal actions clear while preserving other people’s contributions; use I and we according to the supplied facts rather than inflating ownership or hiding an individual role. |
| L06.S03 | Explain Why You Made a Decision | 4:30 | Explain the information and uncertainty behind a decision, keeping the participant’s stated reasoning separate from later observations or unproved causal outcomes. |
| L06.S04 | Describe Results Without Overclaiming | 4:30 | Distinguish a completed action, a directly observed outcome and an unsupported wider or causal claim when describing an experience. |
| L06.S05 | Reflect and Answer the Question Asked | 4:30 | Give a reflection grounded in the case and respond to the particular follow-up asked, distinguishing future intentions from completed actions and unknown details. |
| L06.S06 | Rehearse a Truthful Account | 5:30 | Privately rehearse a clear account grounded in what is known, distinguish personal contributions and evidence limits, and prepare to answer a specific follow-up without inventing details. |
| L06.S07 | Adapt the Same Experience to a New Question | 3:00 | Select different relevant evidence from the same experience for a changed follow-up, without changing its facts, ownership or uncertainty. |

Total editorial allocation: 30 minutes. Derived English silent reading estimate: **1752.667 seconds**, using 140 spoken words per minute, a 0.35-second tail per cue, and the explicit holds. This is not a measured animation or a promise about the future voiced runtime. Do not add filler to force 30:00.

S02 and S03 contain authored choice gates about contribution and reasoning. S06 contains self-paced private preparation and rehearsal of a truthful personal account. The other sections share reflection and explanation in both modes. Watch chooses one declared branch at each gate; it does not play every mutually exclusive branch. Practice time includes unbounded learner time and depends on the selected branch.

The same explicitly fictional visitor-guide case supplies the finite facts across all seven sections. S07 changes the follow-up question rather than inventing another achievement. The model may illustrate language and structure, but it must not become a learner biography. Keep observation, personal reasoning, unknown impact and a proposed future change distinct.
