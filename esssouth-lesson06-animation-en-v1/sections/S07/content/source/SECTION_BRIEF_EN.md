## L06.S07.B04: Carry the method into your account

Scene family: closing checklist without scoring

### L06.S07.c006 · coach

If you prepared your own account, try one different follow-up now. Select relevant details you can honestly describe; leave other details available for later. If you are using the fictional case for language practice, keep that label. A model gives you a way to practise, not a personal history.
