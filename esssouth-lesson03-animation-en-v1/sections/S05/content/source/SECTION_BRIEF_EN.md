## L03.S05.B04: Retain the Shared Constraints

Scene family: constraint_check

### L03.S05.c008 · coach

Keep the task visible when you respond. Adding another facilitator would change the stated conditions; making the demonstration fill the session would remove time to try. A proposal must be checked against the resources and the purpose, not only against one concern.

**Visual:** Show the fixed one-facilitator card and a thirty-minute rail. Place these counterfactual changes in a separate comparison lane, not the actual plan.

**Phrase anchor:** resources and the purpose

### L03.S05.c009 · coach

**Visual:** Keep a small conditional Ask whether change is possible note separate from the unchanged actual task.

**Phrase anchor:** Do not silently assume
